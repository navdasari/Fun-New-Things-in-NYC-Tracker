Indian & Asian Restaurants
8 new
Badshah Social
Park Slope · Indian
From a chef who previously ran a Hell's Kitchen spot of the same name. Crispy spinach chaat, Amritsari fish, and lamb shank nihari.
Canto Blu
Borough Park · Cantonese
A cavernous dim sum destination with a full dumpling lineup plus a separate sushi and raw fish menu.
Man Fire Yakitori
Flushing · Japanese
Pick your own skewers — ribeye, duck tongue, flounder head — and watch them grill over charcoal. Open until 2am.
Mūje
Chelsea · Pan-Asian
An eight-course, $150 tasting menu from the Jungsik team, sharing space with sibling restaurant SEA — think yellowtail with fermented tomato and shrimp toast with caviar.
Roll Over
Union Square · Korean
Counter-service kimbap with fillings like crispy pork cutlet and braised beef shank, plus tteokbokki — part of NYC's growing kimbap wave.
Sushi Roll Club
Crown Heights · Japanese
A sit-down follow-up to the counter-only Silver Rice, now with real tables and plates — rolls, chirashi bowls, and sashimi platters.
Lady Pho
Williamsburg · Vietnamese
Casual Vietnamese staples, including a phở built with a whole bone-in short rib and an avocado milkshake for dessert.
Taco Mahal
Upper East Side · Indian-Latin
A mini-chain crossing Indian and Latin American food — papadum chips instead of tortilla, plus biryani, paneer tikka masala, and tacos on naan or roti.
Cocktail & Wine Bars
7 new
Dokidoki
Williamsburg · Cocktail bar
A Latin-Japanese cocktail bar pairing a shochu cafecito with low-ABV drinks, from a bartender pairing San Francisco and NYC bar pedigree.
Maison Madison
Financial District · Cocktail bar
Parisian-inspired, with a weekday 4–6pm happy hour: affordable prosecco, negronis, and escargot at the bar.
FKA
East Village · Cocktail bar
Playful East Village newcomer serving dirty shirleys and espresso martinis alongside dollar oysters and hot dogs.
The Museum Bar + Gift Shop
Bushwick · Cocktail bar
Natural-history-themed, right down to an ant-and-cricket negroni and dino nuggets on the menu.
Only Child
Bed-Stuy · Cocktail bar
From the Frog Wine Bar team, open until 2am on weekends — martinis, popcorn, and clam dip.
Wallace
Prospect Heights · Wine bar
A cozy wine bar with backyard seating, martinis, and a small snacky menu of gildas and oysters.
Le Dive
West Village · Wine bar
The Dimes Square wine bar's second location — martinis, a compact French snack menu, and plenty of sidewalk seating for people-watching.

Counter-service sibling of San Babila next door — focaccia, croissants (including Nutella), and pistachio froyo served in a croissant cone.
